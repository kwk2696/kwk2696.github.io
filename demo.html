Bimanual Cube Sorting

2× AgileX Piper · 2026.09.21

Two Piper arms sorting three cubes onto plates, with the top-down camera and both wrist views shown side by side. Each arm runs in its own ROS domain under one shared workspace. An LLM turns instructions into a sequential program (Code-as-Policies); rule-based routing assigns arms and inserts arm-to-arm handovers, and orchestration decides what runs in parallel. Because these layers are separate, changing arm count or collision checks leaves the rest untouched.
